01 · POWER GENERATION

Power generation with gas engines.

Reliable electricity generation based on gas reciprocating engines for efficient, flexible and continuous power supply.

50 kW – 12 MW Single-unit output
Up to 50 % Electrical efficiency
3 min To full load
TECHNOLOGY

Flexible power generation for different energy requirements.

AES develops power generation solutions based on high-performance gas engines. The systems can be used for continuous electricity generation as well as flexible operating modes.

Gas reciprocating engines operate according to the Otto or Miller cycle and enable efficient utilisation of different gaseous fuels.

APPLICATIONS
  • Continuous power generation
  • Grid support
  • Peak load operation
  • Backup and reserve power
  • Hybrid energy systems
02 · CHP & TRIGENERATION

Heat and power from cogeneration.

Combined production of electricity and useful heat, with the possibility of integrated cooling production.

50 kW – 9.5 MW Unit output
>95 % Total efficiency
TECHNOLOGY

Efficient production of electricity, heat and cooling.

AES cogeneration systems combine electricity generation with the utilisation of useful heat.

Depending on the application, the recovered heat can be supplied as hot water or steam, while integrated trigeneration systems can also provide cooling.

SYSTEM DESIGN
  • Modular execution
  • Containerised execution
  • Central control systems
  • Different fuel options
  • Heat recovery systems
03 · GAS & FUEL SOLUTIONS

Gas purification and preparation systems.

Gas purification and preparation systems for associated gas, flare gas and other special gaseous fuels.

TECHNOLOGY

Preparing difficult gases for reliable power generation.

Associated and flare gases can contain hydrogen sulphide, heavy hydrocarbons and other components that affect engine operation.

AES gas purification and preparation systems condition the fuel gas for use in gas reciprocating power plants.

APPLICATIONS
  • Natural gas
  • Associated gas
  • Flare gas
  • Biogas
  • Other special gases
04 · MODULAR SYSTEMS

Modular & containerised power plants.

Modular power plant solutions enable rapid implementation, compact installation and flexible expansion of plant capacity.

TECHNOLOGY

Prefabricated modules for flexible implementation.

AES block-modular power plants are built from prefabricated container blocks in which the required equipment is already integrated.

The modular design allows plants to be installed where infrastructure is limited and expanded with additional modules as energy demand increases.

ADVANTAGES

Compact. Fast. Expandable.

  • Prefabricated container blocks
  • Short implementation times
  • Compact design
  • Fast assembly and disassembly
  • Flexible capacity expansion
05 · GRID SUPPORT

Grid support & backup power.

Flexible power supply for baseload, grid support, peak load and fast reserve operation.

3 min Fast start-up
OPERATION MODES

One system. Multiple operating modes.

AES power plants can be configured for continuous baseload operation, grid support, peak load operation and backup power.

Gas reciprocating engines enable fast ramp-up and ramp-down and can provide rapid reserve capacity when required.

FLEXIBILITY
  • Baseload operation
  • Grid support
  • Peak load operation
  • Backup power
  • Hybrid operation
